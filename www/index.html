↻ Renovate your old resume

Already have a resume? Upload it as a PDF — or paste its full text below. The details fill themselves automatically — as soon as you paste or the PDF loads, CareerGlow reads it and fills your name, contact, job title, jobs, education, skills and the other sections it finds straight into the form below (filled fields flash gold, so you can check them). Anything the old resume doesn't mention stays exactly as you left it. You can still edit every filled field, or tap Renovate to apply it again.

PDF tip: text-based PDFs work best. If your PDF is a scanned image with no selectable text, open it, copy any readable text, and paste it above instead.

Auto-fill is on by default: paste text, drop a file, or load a PDF and the matching fields below fill themselves — no extra tap needed. Turn it off if you prefer to fill only when you tap Renovate.
